# Changelog

## 0.1.4

- The trinket and its effects are translated into every language Valheim offers except Abenaki, and
  follow the game's language setting.
- Players on 0.1.x can still play together.

## 0.1.3

- Bug reports and feedback now go to the #spearbreaker channel on Discord: https://discord.gg/fBtGdJ6YSs
- Players on 0.1.x can still play together.

## 0.1.2

- The trinket is now called Spearbreaker, and its icon is the Heart of the Forest's in blue-grey tin.
- The effects are renamed Gungnir's Aim (while worn) and Odin's Claim (full adrenaline), with icons
  in the same tint.

## 0.1.1

- Mod icon recoloured to blue-grey tin to match the charm.

## 0.1.0

- First release: the Spearbreaker charm, a blue-grey tin Forge trinket (5 Bronze, 2 Troll trophies, 3 Rubies).
  While worn, spear hits treat creatures as one step weaker to pierce (resistant becomes normal,
  normal becomes weak, weak becomes very weak). At full adrenaline, spear hits deal 100% more
  pierce damage for 30 s.
  Duration, adrenaline cost and damage bonus are configurable and synced from the server.
