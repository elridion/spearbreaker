# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: the Spearbreaker charm, a Forge trinket (5 Bronze, 2 Troll trophies, 3 Rubies).
  While worn, spear hits treat creatures as one step weaker to pierce (resistant becomes normal,
  normal becomes weak, weak becomes very weak). At full adrenaline, spear hits deal 100% more
  pierce damage for 30 s.
  Duration, adrenaline cost and damage bonus are configurable and synced from the server.
