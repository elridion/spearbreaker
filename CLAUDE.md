# Spearbreaker

Valheim mod adding one trinket, the **Spearbreaker charm** (display names: item "Spearbreaker",
passive "Gungnir's Aim", active "Odin's Claim"). While worn, its passive `SE_SpearbreakerPassive`
makes spear hits (melee and thrown) treat a creature as one step weaker to pierce; at full adrenaline
`SE_Spearbreaker` adds extra pierce damage for a while. Vanilla spears and bows share the pierce
type; this gives spears their own answer without adding a damage type.

Stack, build, verification and git workflow are shared by all mods: see `../CLAUDE.md` and
`../.claude/rules/`.

## Where things are

- `src/SpearbreakerItem.cs` registers the trinket (clone of `TrinketBronzeHealth`, the Heart of the
  Forest), recipe, recolour and icons; `src/SE_SpearbreakerPassive.cs` and `src/SE_Spearbreaker.cs`
  the two effects; `src/DamagePatches.cs` the resistance shift.
- `assets/icon.png` fallback icon (embedded as `Spearbreaker.icon.png`); `tools/make_icons.ps1`
  generates it and `package/icon.png`.

## Invariants that must not break

- **Saved-world compatibility.** Persisted or networked identifiers: plugin GUID
  `elridion.spearbreaker`, item prefab `TrinketSpearbreaker` (saved in inventories and chests), and
  status effect names `SE_Spearbreaker` and `SE_SpearbreakerPassive` (synced by name hash).
- **The attribute bit** `1 << 20` is part of the network protocol between peers; changing it needs a
  minor version bump. If another mod is found using it, move it and bump.
- **Vanilla assets are shared.** Never modify the Heart of the Forest prefab or its SharedData; config
  changes write only the clone's SharedData, which every crafted charm shares.
- **Every peer needs the mod:** the resistance shift runs on whichever peer owns the creature.
- **Docs follow code.** In the same change, `package/README.md` keeps the recipe in sync with
  `SpearbreakerItem`, the settings table and defaults with `SpearbreakerPlugin`, and the effect
  description with `SE_Spearbreaker` and `DamagePatches`.
